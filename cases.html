Remote Community Connectivity

Problem

No Reliable Internet Access

Remote communities had limited or no reliable internet access, directly impacting education, health services, and daily communication. Existing infrastructure was either non-existent or too unreliable to depend on.

Solution

Public Wi-Fi + Starlink Satellite Backhaul

Design of a public Wi-Fi network with Starlink satellite backhaul — optimised for remote community use with content filtering, access controls, and solar-powered operation.

Implementation

Mesh Network + Solar Deployment

Deployment of multiple Starlink systems across community zones, installation of outdoor mesh wireless infrastructure, solar and battery-powered equipment for off-grid operation, and community-focused access management including captive portals and content filtering.

Result

Connectivity delivered across multiple community zones. Hundreds of users gained reliable internet access. Fully operational within weeks of deployment. Significant improvement in service availability and uptime.
