Core Capabilities

What we do best — the services that define every major project we deliver.

Public Wi-Fi & Community Connectivity

Reliable public Wi-Fi networks designed for remote and regional communities, including First Nations locations. Covers site design, outdoor wireless infrastructure, captive portals, content filtering, and integration with satellite backhaul.

Remote Network Infrastructure

End-to-end delivery of resilient network infrastructure built for remote, harsh, and infrastructure-limited environments — including solar-powered and off-grid solutions.

Telecommunications & Network Solutions

Scalable data and telecommunications infrastructure for government agencies and enterprise operators — designed for reliability and long-term performance across complex environments.

Satellite Connectivity

High-performance Starlink-based connectivity for remote and underserved locations — integrated with local network infrastructure, with redundancy and failover built in.
